Before you start

Clear answers to the main questions.

What happens after I request access?

Duvela reviews your teacher request and sends a demo/TestFlight invitation. After approval, you can test the teacher hub and prepare your first offer.

What can I publish?

Courses, LIVE lessons and events such as workshops, clubs or online sessions.

Do I need to install anything?

The teacher workspace and LIVE tools open in the browser.

Can learners see my profile?

Yes. The public profile can present your subjects, languages, experience, links, courses and upcoming events.

Can I manage assignments?

The teacher workspace supports reviewing speaking and writing submissions, scores and written feedback.

Is Duvela only for independent teachers?

No. The platform also supports course creators, organizers and schools working with classes and events.
