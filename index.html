    Meet DUVI's friends

    DUVI stays the main guide. When a learner needs speaking, grammar, stories, pronunciation, motivation, or exam support, the right buddy steps in.

    ONE TEAM. YOUR PROGRESS.

    One assistant, seven specialist buddies

    Open DUVI, tell the assistant what you need, and you'll be routed to the best helper. Each buddy has a clear job, simple tasks, and a friendly style, so you always get the right kind of support at the right moment.

    1. NOVAHear one short phrase and repeat it.
    2. LINADo a tiny speaking sprint or roleplay.
    3. GRAMIFix one mistake and explain the rule.
    4. STELLAGet a mini story with easy vocabulary.
    5. MOTIProtect the streak and reward progress.
    6. ELSAPrepare for exam speaking and better answers.
    7. SOFIAPractice live-style speaking with warm guidance.

    LINA

    Speaking buddy

    Short dialogues, roleplay, confidence drills, and first words out loud from day one.

    🎤 Speaking 🗣 Roleplay

    GRAMI

    Grammar buddy

    Simple corrections, grammar patterns, articles, verb forms, and clear next-step explanations.

    ✅ Corrections 📘 Rules

    STELLA

    Story buddy

    Micro stories, simple scenes, and short dialogue moments that turn vocabulary into memory.

    ✨ Stories 📖 Reading

    NOVA

    Pronunciation buddy

    Listening checks, sound practice, repeat-after-me drills, and phrase-by-phrase pronunciation work.

    🎧 Listening 🔊 Pronunciation

    MOTI

    Motivation buddy

    Streak support, rewards, encouragement, mini challenges, and the next small win to chase.

    🔥 Streaks 🏆 Rewards
